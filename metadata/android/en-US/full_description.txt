Simple Reminders is a no-fuss app for the things you do on repeat — medications, plants, pets, chores, habits. Set a reminder once and let the app track whether it got done.

Ever wonder "Did I fill the cat feeder? Water the plants? Take my meds?" Every occurrence is marked done or missed, and it works fully offline with no account.

The calendar is the home screen: each day shows what's done, pending or missed, so you can see the week at a glance.

• Repeat on chosen weekdays, every N days, weeks, months or years, or just once — with multiple times per day
• Snooze, pause, or archive without losing history
• Duplicate a reminder to make a variant in seconds
• Importance levels control how insistent the notification is, each with its own tone, or pick one per reminder
• Missed summary after a restart
• 100 icons and 8 theme packs, light and dark
• Reminders survive reboots

100% offline. No account, no ads, no analytics. Everything stays on your device.
